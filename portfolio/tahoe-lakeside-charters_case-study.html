Overview

Your lake.
Your pace.

Tahoe Lakeside Charters came to us as a locally owned, family operated boat charter company based in South Lake Tahoe, ready to move their booking experience online. They needed a site that felt as relaxed and personal as the trips themselves, while still making it dead simple for a visitor to pick a cruise and book it.

The challenge was balancing warmth with clarity. Every section, from the trip packages to the captain's story, was built to answer one question: why charter with this crew instead of a crowded tour boat. The result is a booking focused site that showcases the Formula 330 SS, introduces Captain Chris Davis, and moves visitors from scenic daydream to a confirmed reservation.

Process

How it came together

The build moved through three focused phases, each grounded in the charter company's core promise: relaxed, scenic Lake Tahoe experiences for families, couples, and small groups.

01
Discovery

We mapped the trip lineup across five packages, from a quick 2 hour pleasure cruise to a full 8 hour charter, plus the Emerald Bay sightseeing routes and sunset cruises. Each package needed its own page with clear duration, inclusions, and a fast path to booking.

02
Design

A calm, coastal aesthetic was built around deep lake blues and clean typography to reflect the family owned, safety first feel of the business. Trip cards, the boat and captain story, and the Book Now button were all placed to guide a browsing visitor toward a reservation.

03
Development

Built on WordPress with WPBakery for flexible content management, a dedicated booking flow, and full mobile optimization. Trip package pages, the boat and captain profile, and an FAQ section were all structured to answer a guest's questions before they ever call.

Trip Packages

Five ways.
One beautiful lake.

The full lineup covers everything from a quick sightseeing run to a full day on the water, built for guests who just want to relax and take in the view.

01
2 Hour Pleasure Cruise

Short on time but ready for some serious Tahoe fun. Cruise the lake, take in the scenery of North Tahoe, head out for sightseeing, or drop anchor for a swim. Two hours, your crew, one beautiful lake.

02
Emerald Bay Sightseeing Cruise

A 2 hour, relaxed, scenic private cruise to one of Lake Tahoe's most iconic destinations. Perfect for first time visitors and families with children who want the classic Tahoe view.

03
Emerald Bay Sightseeing & Swim Cruise

A 4 hour extended charter that lets guests take Emerald Bay at a slower pace, with scenic cruising plus time to anchor, swim, and enjoy the water.

04
Sunset Cruise

A 2 or 4 hour cruise timed for the moment the sun dips behind the mountains and the lake turns gold and pink. Peaceful, romantic, and perfect for slowing down.

05
Special Occasion Cruise

A custom route built around your celebration, whether it's a birthday, anniversary, proposal, or just a day worth remembering on the water.
